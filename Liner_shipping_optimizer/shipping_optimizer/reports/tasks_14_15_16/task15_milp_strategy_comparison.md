## 3. Defect found: a time-limited incumbent can be reported as *unverified*

At the 120 s limit the CBC incumbent for seed 1 carried **4 vessels more than the plan needs** (`n_s > ceil(T f / 7)`;
the constraint is only a lower bound and the solver stopped before removing the slack). The independent ledger charges the
minimum, so MILP objective 7,257,741 ≠ ledger profit 8,321,741 (Δ = 1,064,000 USD/wk) and `plan.reconciliation.reconciled`
was **false** — the whole global stage would have been `verified: false`, the run `incomplete_accounting`, and an
iteration like it could never beat a verified one, although the plan was feasible and the ledger profit correct. It
happened on 1 of 5 controlled seeds at the production limit (none of the ~14 recorded end-to-end orchestrator runs of this
and the previous mission, all `status: complete`; those runs additionally receive regional hints). Shorter limits make it common (30 s smoke
run: −1.015 M on seed 0).

**Fix (patch A, `evidence/patches/task15_A_incumbent_vessel_trim.patch`)**: after extracting an incumbent,
`_trim_slack_vessels` sets each service's vessels to the minimum the vessel constraint needs. It cannot weaken a
constraint (`7n ≥ T f` holds with equality; per-class fleet limits only get looser), changes no frequency or flow, and the
amount removed is reported (`reconciliation.vessel_slack_removed` / `vessel_slack_cost_usd`). Evidence: on the same seed
the patched tree reports MILP objective = ledger profit = 8,321,741, `reconciled` true, 4 vessels / 1,064,000 USD removed;
the other four seeds are bit-identical to the baseline (0 removed). End to end the defect also appeared in a real
pipeline run once the pool changed: in `T16g_orch_econ_run1` (economic filter) iterations 1 and 2 returned incumbents
with **3 and 1 slack vessels**, and iteration 1 was the selected one — without patch A its reconciliation would have
failed by the slack cost and the run would have ended `incomplete_accounting`; with it the run is `complete`, verified.
Tests: `tests/test_incumbent_vessel_trim.py` (4;
all four fail on the baseline source — "assert 5 == 3": five vessels reported where three are needed).

## 3b. Defect found by the release gate: solver noise reported as a flow (patch C)

First gate round with the HiGHS backend (`T15_orch_highs_run3`): **341/342**. The failing assertion was the test's own
independent recomputation: `flow_on_inactive_service` for two ODs routed over `asia_svc_123` / `asia_svc_255`, which the
plan does not operate. The flows were **1.1e-9 and 1.0e-9 TEU** — constraint-tolerance noise (`q ≤ capacity × f` with
`f = 0` is satisfied within ~1e-7 by both solvers), included because the extraction cut-off was `> 1e-9`. The pipeline's own
ledger tolerates ≤ 1e-6 and reported no violation, so the assertion was the only thing that saw it.

Fix (patch C): `FLOW_NOISE_TEU = 1e-6` — a routed quantity at or below it is noise, not a flow. It does **not** hide real
violations: a flow above 1e-6 TEU on an inactive service is still reported by the ledger (and the test). Regression test
`test_solver_noise_is_not_reported_as_a_flow_on_an_inactive_service` injects 1.5e-9 into every zero of the HiGHS result and
fails on the pre-fix source ("assert False") — the objective-vs-ledger reconciliation tolerance (≥ 1 USD) is unaffected.
The second gate round (§7) passed 342/342 on all three HiGHS runs. Per the change-control sequence the first failed gate
stopped further adoption work until it was understood and fixed.

### What the evidence says about the three families

**A — better restricted candidate selection (CBC).** The restriction *does* lose useful candidates: in the proven
optima **2, 5, 3, 4, 2 of the 21, 15, 14, 13, 18 operated services (≈ 20 %) are outside the LP support**, carrying
6.5 / 26.6 / 9.3 / 30.4 / 6.5 % of the optimum's revenue. All of them are stand-alone profitable (0.46 – 3.86 M/wk of their
own; 2–5-port Feeder_800 services and a few Post/Super-panamax) — they are not weak feeders sacrificed for network value;
the relaxation simply spreads fractional frequency elsewhere (seed 0: 326 of the 327 support services have LP frequency
< 1, 187 fractional vessels, bound 44.2 M against an optimum of 10.0 M). Enlarging the restricted set with them (`A_sa`,
`A_pert`) is worse in CBC, because CBC already needs ~100 s for the 327-service problem; shrinking it (`A_top*`) solves in
seconds but loses value. **No restriction rule beat the production restriction under CBC.**

**B — controlled unrestricted solve.** With CBC it fails (above). With HiGHS it is the winning strategy: the unrestricted
model (≈ 17.3 k variables, 6.1 k constraints) is solved to proven optimality in 200 – 300 s and to within 0 – 1.2 % of
the optimum in 120 s, and the best incumbent is always kept at the time limit. The comparison isolates the *solver*, not
the formulation: the model, constraints, pool and ledger are identical.

**C — runtime-budget allocation.** A "quick feasible, then improve" scheme only works if the quick stage is genuinely
quick: CBC's first incumbent of the 330-service problem takes tens of seconds (T1 = 40 s: no incumbent on 4/5 seeds), and
warm-starting the unrestricted CBC model with it did not help in the remaining time. The progressive CBC variant keeps the
best incumbent and respects the budget but its gain (+0.08 / +0.19 M) is smaller than the seed-to-seed spread.

**This is a trade-off, reported as one.** The objective of the global stage is profit; the profit-optimal networks serve
slightly less cargo on seeds 0 – 3 (−0.7 pp mean over the five seeds; seed 3: 17.1 % vs 19.2 %) and more on seed 4

vessel hire −1.6 to −2.3 M, fuel −2.4 to −5.2 M — because it drops loss-making coverage; a lower cost caused by serving
less is *not* claimed as an improvement: the claim is the higher verified profit at equal constraints. If a coverage floor is
wanted it is an explicit owner decision (`min_served_fraction` exists in the solver and is unused by the global stage).

## 8. Rollback and rollout

* Patch A (`task15_A_incumbent_vessel_trim.patch`, 3 files) and patch C (`task15_C_solver_noise_flows.patch`, 2 files):
  `git apply -R` of each, or restore `src/network/network_milp.py` and `src/network/plan.py` from snapshot `B0`.
* Patch B is inert unless `NETWORK_MILP_BACKEND=highs`. To adopt it, set that variable for the pipeline (or change the
  default in `network_milp._backend_name`); to roll back, unset it. Fixed-frequency (regional) solves always use CBC.
  Because the Task 16 economic filter is now the default, adopt HiGHS at 120 s **together with**
  `LINER_REGIONAL_FILTER=legacy` (measured 9.31 M), or raise the global-stage budget (`GLOBAL_STAGE_TIME_LIMIT_SEC`).
* Not done, and why: HiGHS as the **default** — a solver-engine change of the global stage; the instruction requires
  approval for a new solver. Availability is proven (scipy 1.12.0 is a pinned requirement and `scipy.optimize.milp` ran in
  this environment; no package is added), but the decision is the owner's.

## 9. Limitations (nothing hidden)

* The controlled experiment bypasses the regional GA, so the global stage gets no regional hints (production passes 12 – 13
  `f = 1` hints). HiGHS in this interface has no warm start, so hints do not matter to it; for CBC they add a weak warm
  start. The end-to-end orchestrator runs include the hints.
* Time-limited results depend on CPU (§2). HiGHS is less exposed (3/5 pools close before the limit) but not immune.
* Five seeds of one generator; the generator is unseeded in production, so the pipeline as a whole is not bit-reproducible.
* The HiGHS version bundled with scipy 1.12.0 is not exposed by this interface; the version string is the scipy version.
* `A_sa` / `A_pert` rows of `T15_B0matrix_seed*.json` are **invalid** (a harness status bug, found and fixed; the
  numbers used are those of `T15_clean_cbc`).
