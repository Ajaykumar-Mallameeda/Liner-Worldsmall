**Question.** Do the validated pieces — C1 (`LINER_MILP_VUB`), C2 (`LINER_LOCAL_SEARCH`), 10 % class variants (`LINER_CLASS_VARIANTS=0.10`)
— hold up *together*, on hold-out generator seeds 10 – 19 that no earlier decision used, against the frozen default and against the strongest
single change (local search alone)?  And is the one remaining optional piece (search-only variants) worth keeping?
**Solver, budget, defaults:** CBC 2.10.3, 120 s stage budget, single thread; every flag default OFF (the baseline arm below runs the modified
tree with no flag set).

### 2.1 What the table says, and what it does not

1. **The combination beats the frozen default on every one of the 20 seeds** (+0.95 … +6.87 M), 10/10 on the hold-out alone, with higher coverage
   (+4.1 pp), higher margin (+1.6 pp) and a *shorter* stage (114.3 s against 120.7 s).  This is the acceptance statement for the combination
   as a whole: **ACCEPT FOR OWNER APPROVAL** (flags remain default OFF).
2. **Most of that gain is the local search alone** (+2.28 M, 20/20).  What C1 + the class variants add on top of the search is +0.97 M on average
   but is **not uniform**: 14 of 20 seeds higher (dev 6/10, hold-out 8/10); three seeds gain 3.4 – 5.2 M while six lose between 0.02 and 1.25 M.  By the stated
   rule (≥ 8/10) this increment is **inconclusive**, not rejected: the mean is positive and t = 2.7 on 20 seeds, but the rule is not met on
   the development set nor pooled.  The owner therefore has two defensible choices — the minimal one (local search only: one post-solve step,
   no pool and no model change, 20/20) and the combination (+0.97 M more on average, three flags, a larger pool).
3. **The search-only variants (`fin`) do not pass on the hold-out** (7/10; pooled 15/20) although the mean is positive (+0.18 M, ≈ 1 %):
   **EXPERIMENTAL ONLY**.  They also carry the one accounting failure of §3 and the only zero-cargo service found in the gate scan of all arms.
4. Dispersion: the combination's profit has sd 1.16 against 1.30 for the default, and the worst of its 20 plans (12.98 M) is above the
   default's 20-seed mean (11.94 M).
5. Not claimed: that 20 generator seeds of one dataset is a population; that the gain survives the unrevised-vs-revised transit data
   (see `maritime_assumption_validation.md` for the sensitivities that were run); that a different time budget keeps the ranking.

## 3. A defect found by the gate on the hold-out, and its fix

**What the gate showed.**  `H5_fin_seed17` (the arm with the search-only variants) is the only one of the 80 solved plans of batches D5 / H5 — and
of every solved plan in the campaign's controlled batches (gate scan, `evidence/comparisons/GATE_SCAN.md`) — whose regional partition did not verify: regional profit 15,726,757 against the ledger's 15,652,245 (+74,513), regional vessel hire 17,955,000
against 17,990,000 (−35,000 = one Feeder_450 vessel-week).  The network ledger itself was valid, with zero violations, and reconciled to the
MILP objective (relative difference 2.4e-16): the *plan* was correct, its *regional split* was not.

**Cause (read from the plan, not inferred).**  The last move of the local search added `lv0086` (Feeder_450, one vessel, f = 1, CNYTN –
TWKHH) for a gain of 9,189 USD/week.  The final re-routing over the full path set then found better paths (+305,529 USD/week), and in that
optimum the new service carries **zero cargo** — a vessel hired for nothing (weekly cost 74,513 USD).  Two independent weaknesses met:

1. *No idle-service pruning.*  A service that carries nothing changes neither cargo nor coverage, so dropping it can only raise profit.
   Nothing did.  (It is the only zero-cargo service in the gate scan of every output of this campaign, ~330 plans.)
2. *Regional attribution of a service without cargo and without a registered owner.*  Search-only variants are modelled inside the plan, after
   the regional pools were collected, so `lv####` services had no owner and no proposers; a cargo-less one fell through the fallback
   (`owner.get(sid)` → `None`) and its cost silently left the regional partition, breaking the stated invariant "only the split between regions
   can change, never the network totals".  (Variants that carried cargo were attributed through their cargo: of the 78 plans in this campaign's outputs
   with an operated `lv` service, 77 verified; the one that did not is this one, and it holds the only cargo-less `lv` service.)

**Fix (source diff in `patches/final_vs_accepted.patch`; tests in `tests/test_local_search.py`).**

| change | file | effect |
|---|---|---|
| `prune_idle_services` after the re-routing and the repair, only when the local search is enabled **and applied** | `src/network/local_search.py`, `plan.py` | drops zero-cargo services; frequencies, vessels and the cost terms of the objective are updated from the same service models the MILP and the ledger use; reported in `local_search.idle_pruned` |
| owners and proposers of `lv####` services are registered from the rotation they are a variant of | `src/network/reconcile.py` (`_register_variant_owners`) | search-only variants take part in the regional split like any candidate |
| a service with no cargo and no known proposer is charged to the first region in the stated order instead of vanishing | `reconcile.py` (`_fallback_owner`) | the regional totals equal the ledger in every case, by construction |

Tests (5 new, all pass; 341 in the changed-module files): an empty service is dropped and profit rises by exactly its fixed cost; nothing is
dropped when every service carries cargo; owner registration (known, chained through a `cv` id, unknown source → no invented owner);
fallback ownership (first proposer → rotation owner → first region → none only without regions); end-to-end regional totals equal the ledger
with search-only variants enabled.  Default behaviour is untouched: the prune runs only inside the enabled search stage.

**Why the earlier D5 / H5 evidence stands.**  The fixes cannot lower any plan's profit (a drop of an idle service adds its fixed cost; the
attribution change moves cost between regions, not out of the network).  Of the 80 plans of D5 / H5 one is affected.  Nevertheless the
hold-out was re-run on the fixed tree (batch C9, §4), so that the final-code statement does not rest on an argument.
